# Live HSE Quiz — Question Bank (200 MCQs)

Medium difficulty, four options, one key, written to PSI item-writing guidelines from the current KOC procedure revisions. Each item cites the clause it was written from. The live quiz shuffles option order, so the key letter below applies to this printed order only.


## KOC.SA.002 — Safe Control and Use of Scaffolding, Portable Ladders and Stepladders (8 items)

**Q001.** According to KOC.SA.002, how often must an in-service scaffold be re-inspected by an authorized Scaffolding Inspector, whether or not it has been used?  
A. Every 7 days  
B. Every 14 days  
C. Every 30 days  
D. Only after alterations or bad weather  
*Key: A — Cl. 4.1.3 / 5.1.6: inspection before first use, after alteration, after adverse weather and every 7 days thereafter.*

**Q002.** Above what wind speed does KOC.SA.002 prohibit work on or from scaffolds?  
A. 40 km/h  
B. 50 km/h  
C. 65 km/h  
D. 80 km/h  
*Key: C — Cl. 5.1.3 c): work on scaffolds is prohibited during storms or high winds (wind speed more than 65 km/h).*

**Q003.** An elevated scaffold work area requires at least two means of exit when the horizontal travel distance to the nearest exit ladder exceeds:  
A. 10 m  
B. 15 m  
C. 20 m  
D. 30 m  
*Key: B — Cl. 5.1.3 f): where horizontal travel distance to the nearest exit exceeds 15 m (50 ft), at least two means of exit are required.*

**Q004.** A worker is setting up a portable ladder against a structure. Which set-up ratio meets the KOC.SA.002 requirement of approximately 76 degrees?  
A. 1 m out for every 2 m up  
B. 1 m out for every 3 m up  
C. 1 m out for every 4 m up  
D. 1 m out for every 6 m up  
*Key: C — Cl. 5.2.2 h): ladders shall be set at an angle of 76 degrees (1 m out for 4 m up).*

**Q005.** Where no adequate handhold is provided at the landing, a portable ladder must extend above the landing place by at least:  
A. 0.60 m  
B. 0.90 m  
C. 1.05 m  
D. 1.50 m  
*Key: C — Cl. 5.2.2 i): the ladder must rise at least 1.05 m (3.5 ft) above the landing place.*

**Q006.** Without a supporting risk assessment, what is the minimum distance a scaffold may be erected from an existing excavation?  
A. 1 m  
B. 1.5 m  
C. 2 m  
D. 3 m  
*Key: D — Cl. 5.1.3 aa): no scaffold shall be erected less than 3 m from an existing excavation; a shorter distance only based on a risk assessment.*

**Q007.** What minimum experience does KOC.SA.002 require for a Scaffolding Supervisor, in addition to certified erection and dismantling training?  
A. 3 years in scaffolding, with no supervisory requirement  
B. 5 years in scaffolding, including at least 1 year as a supervisor  
C. 5 years in scaffolding, including at least 3 years as a supervisor  
D. 10 years in scaffolding, including at least 2 years as a supervisor  
*Key: B — Cl. 5.1.2 b): minimum 5 years' experience in scaffolding jobs, of which at least one year in a supervisory role.*

**Q008.** What is the maximum permitted height of a portable stepladder under KOC.SA.002?  
A. 3.0 m (10 ft)  
B. 4.5 m (15 ft)  
C. 6.1 m (20 ft)  
D. 9.0 m (30 ft)  
*Key: C — Cl. 5.2.3 f): portable stepladders shall not exceed 20 ft (6.1 m) in height.*

## KOC.SA.005 — Respiratory Protective Equipment (RPE) Procedure (8 items)

**Q019.** During a routine check, an SCBA breathing air cylinder gauge shows it has dropped below full charge. At what point does KOC.SA.005 require it to be sent for recharging?  
A. When pressure falls to 90% of the specified pressure  
B. When pressure falls to 75% of the specified pressure  
C. When pressure falls to 50% of the specified pressure  
D. When the low-pressure alarm sounds  
*Key: A — Cl. 4.2 / 5.4.3: cylinder pressure must not be less than 90% of full capacity; refill when pressure drops to 90% of specified pressure.*

**Q020.** When checking an SCBA, the cylinder valve is closed slowly to test the low-pressure warning alarm. At approximately what level of cylinder capacity should the alarm sound?  
A. 10%  
B. 25%  
C. 50%  
D. 90%  
*Key: B — Cl. 5.4.5 C: verify activation of the low pressure alarm (to sound at approximately 25% of cylinder capacity).*

**Q021.** What is the maximum validity of an RPE 'Authorized User Card' issued by the Controlling Team?  
A. 6 months  
B. 12 months  
C. 24 months  
D. 36 months  
*Key: B — Cl. 5.4.4: validity of the Authorized User Card shall not exceed 12 months and must be within the medical examination validity.*

**Q022.** What periodic hydrostatic test intervals does KOC.SA.005 specify for SCBA breathing air cylinders?  
A. Steel every 3 years; composite every 5 years  
B. Steel every 5 years; composite every 3 years  
C. Steel and composite both every 5 years  
D. Steel and composite both every 10 years  
*Key: B — Cl. 5.4.5 C: verify SCBA cylinder is current on hydrostatic test (5 years for steel, 3 years for composite cylinders).*

**Q023.** A worker passes a quantitative fit test with a full-face respirator. Which requirement then applies on the job?

B. Any size of the same model may be used

D. Any tight-fitting respirator with the same APF may be used  
*Key: C — Cl. 5.7: after passing a fit test, the user must use the exact same make, model, style and size of respirator; fit testing is at least annual.*

**Q024.** What minimum specification does KOC.SA.005 set for SCBA used by emergency services (Fire Group)?

B. Positive pressure type, minimum 30 minutes duration  
C. Positive pressure type, minimum 45 minutes duration  
D. Positive pressure type, minimum 60 minutes duration  
*Key: C — Cl. 5.2.1 Note 1: SCBA for emergency services shall be positive pressure type with minimum 45 minutes duration, conforming to NFPA 1981.*

**Q025.** An air-purifying respirator filter has been removed from its sealed packaging but rarely used. When must it be replaced, regardless of use?  
A. After 1 month  
B. After 3 months  
C. After 6 months

**Q026.** How often, at a minimum, must the output air quality of a breathing air compressor be checked?

## KOC.SA.007 — Entry into Confined Spaces (9 items)

**Q027.** Which set of gas test results permits confined space entry WITHOUT respiratory protection under KOC.SA.007 Table 1?  
A. O2 19.5–23.5%, flammables < 10% LEL, H2S < 10 ppm  
B. O2 18–23.5%, flammables < 20% LEL, H2S < 10 ppm  
C. O2 19.5–23.5%, flammables < 25% LEL, H2S < 20 ppm  
D. O2 16–21%, flammables < 10% LEL, H2S < 100 ppm  
*Key: A — Table 1: entry without respiratory protection when O2 is 19.5–23.5%, flammables < 10% LEL and H2S < 10 ppm.*

**Q028.** Gas testing of a vessel shows 15% oxygen. According to KOC.SA.007 Table 1, what is the correct action?  
A. Entry permitted with an air-purifying respirator  
B. Entry permitted with forced ventilation only  
C. Entry permitted with SCBA and continuous monitoring  
D. No entry, except in an emergency or for rescue  
*Key: D — Table 1: oxygen content less than 16% (or H2S > 100 ppm, or flammables 50% LEL or above) = NO ENTRY except emergency or rescue.*

**Q029.** The initial confined space gas test was done at 08:00 but entry is delayed until 09:15. What does KOC.SA.007 require?  
A. Entry may proceed because the test is valid for the shift  
B. A follow-up gas test by the Worksite Supervisor before entry  
C. A new Confined Space Entry Certificate must be raised  
D. Entry may proceed if entrants wear personal H2S monitors  
*Key: B — Cl. 5.3: entry is to take place within one hour of the initial gas test; otherwise a follow-up gas test by the Worksite Supervisor is required before entry.*

**Q030.** Where periodic (rather than continuous) gas monitoring is used during a confined space entry, the interval between tests shall not exceed:  
A. 30 minutes  
B. 1 hour  
C. 2 hours  
D. 4 hours  
*Key: C — Cl. 5.13: the periodic monitoring of gas shall not exceed a two-hour period.*

**Q031.** How frequently does KOC.SA.007 require personnel working inside a vessel or tank to be given a rest period, unless conditions require more frequent breaks?  
A. Every 30 minutes  
B. Every 45 minutes  
C. Every 60 minutes  
D. Every 2 hours  
*Key: A — Cl. 5.11: personnel working inside the vessel / tank will be given a rest period at each 30 minute interval, or more frequently.*

**Q032.** Inside a KOC hydrocarbon facility, an excavation is classified as a confined space when its depth exceeds:  
A. 0.6 m  
B. 1.0 m  
C. 1.2 m  
D. 1.5 m  
*Key: C — Cl. 2.0 c): excavation with depth more than 1.2 m inside hydrocarbon facilities is a confined space.*

**Q033.** An entrant collapses inside a vessel. What is the correct immediate action for the standby person who is not trained in search and rescue?  
A. Enter at once wearing an escape set and pull the entrant out  
B. Summon aid via the Control Room and stay outside until relief arrives  
C. Send another entrant inside to assist the casualty to the manway  
D. Lower a spare SCBA set to the casualty and wait at the manway

**Q034.** Which of the following must NEVER be used to ventilate a confined space?

**Q035.** A vessel has been certified gas free but flammable residues may remain. If extra-low-voltage portable lighting is used, what is its maximum voltage under KOC.SA.007?  
A. 12 V AC  
B. 25 V AC  
C. 110 V AC  
D. 240 V AC  
*Key: B — Cl. 5.6: such spaces may be illuminated by extra low voltage (25 V AC) portable lighting approved for Zone 1; transformers never taken inside.*

## KOC.SA.015 — Breaking of Flanges and Bolting Replacement, Rework on Pipework (8 items)

**Q052.** In-service flange bolting replacement (hot bolting) is NOT permitted on flanges having fewer than how many bolts?  
A. Four  
B. Six  
C. Eight  
D. Twelve  
*Key: B — Cl. 5.3: in-service bolt replacement / rework is not permitted on flanges with less than six bolts.*

**Q053.** Above what line pressure does KOC.SA.015 prohibit in-service flange bolting replacement?  
A. 3.5 bar (50 psi)  
B. 6.9 bar (100 psi)  
C. 13.6 bar (200 psi)  
D. 20.7 bar (300 psi)  
*Key: C — Cl. 5.3: not permitted on high-pressure lines > 13.6 bar (200 psi) including air, nitrogen and hydrocarbons.*

**Q054.** A gas line contains 800 ppm of H2S. Under KOC.SA.015, can in-service hot bolting be carried out on it?  
A. Yes, if all workers wear SCBA  
B. Yes, if line pressure is first reduced  
C. No, it is in highly sour service  
D. No, any line above 10 ppm is excluded  
*Key: C — Cl. 2.0 / 5.3: highly sour service is > 500 ppm H2S; hot bolting is not permitted on systems in highly sour service.*

**Q055.** During in-service bolt replacement on a live flange, how many bolts may be slackened at any one time?  
A. One bolt only  
B. Two adjacent bolts  
C. Two diagonally opposite bolts  
D. Up to one quarter of the bolts  
*Key: A — Cl. 5.3 d): slacken one bolt only; under no circumstances must two bolts be slackened at any one time.*

**Q056.** When fitting new bolts during in-service bolt replacement (without tensioning equipment), how far may the bolt protrude beyond the nut?  
A. Flush with the nut  
B. No more than two full threads  
C. No more than five full threads  
D. At least 25 mm  
*Key: B — Cl. 5.3 h): new bolts should protrude no more than two full threads beyond the nut.*

**Q057.** While breaking a flange, the crew finds the bolts are badly corroded and will not turn. What is the only acceptable removal method under KOC.SA.015?  
A. Gas cutting with an oxy-acetylene torch  
B. Grinding with an angle grinder  
C. Cutting with a pneumatic hacksaw  
D. Striking the nuts with an iron hammer  
*Key: C — Cl. 5.4: if bolts are corroded, the only acceptable method is a pneumatic hacksaw; gas cutting (open flame) shall not be used.*

**Q058.** What is the correct body position and first bolt to loosen when breaking a flange that may still contain pressure?  
A. Stand downwind and loosen the bolts nearest to you  
B. Stand upwind and loosen the bolts farthest away from you  
C. Stand directly in front and loosen bolts clockwise  
D. Stand upwind and loosen all bolts evenly at the same time  
*Key: B — Cl. 5.2 o) / 5.5 a): maintain a safe upwind position and loosen bolts farthest away first so any release sprays away from the worker.*

**Q059.** Which type of hammer may be used when breaking flanges on lines in hydrocarbon service?  
A. Iron hammer  
B. Steel sledge hammer  
C. Brass hammer  
D. Any hammer, if a fire watch is present  
*Key: C — Cl. 5.4: do not use an iron hammer in hydrocarbon service because impact will generate sparks; only brass hammers shall be used.*
